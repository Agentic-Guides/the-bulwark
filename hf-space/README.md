# 🛡️ THE BULWARK

**Your home has rules. Now your AI has to follow them.**

A guard MCP for **Alexa+**. Before any add-on is trusted with a lock, a card, or
money, it checks what the add-on actually asks for — and lets the family say
yes or no, out loud.

- 🟢 Scan a harmless add-on → it passes.
- 🔴 Scan a poisoned add-on → it's blocked, with reasons, and pinned to a tamper-evident ledger.

This is the **real deterministic detector** (JS port of `bulwark/detector.py`),
running live in your browser.

**MCP spec 2025-11-25 · Streamable HTTP · optional Amazon Bedrock layer (AWS Builder)**
